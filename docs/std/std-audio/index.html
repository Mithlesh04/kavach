What the spec lists

Function or methodGives
audio.decode(bytes) -> Sound ! AudioErrorThe sound in the bytes of a WAV file (whole-number samples of 8, 16, 24 or 32 bits, or 32-bit floats) or an Ogg Vorbis file, told apart by their first bytes
audio.sound(samples, rate, channels) -> SoundA sound of samples, a [Float32] each from -1 to 1, at rate frames a second (1,000 to 384,000), with 1 or 2 channels; with two, left and right take turns
sound.rate, sound.channels, sound.frames: IntIts frames a second, its channels, and how many frames (one sample of each channel) it has
sound.seconds() -> FloatHow long it lasts
audio.play(sound, volume: 1.0, pan: 0.0, looped: false) -> Voice ! AudioErrorStarts playing sound and gives at once: volume 0 is silent, 1 the sound as it is (up to 4); pan -1 is left, 1 right; looped plays it over and over. Needs Audio
audio.stream(path, volume: 1.0, pan: 0.0, looped: false) -> Voice ! AudioErrorPlays the WAV or Ogg Vorbis file at path as play plays a sound, reading it a piece at a time: for long music, which decode would hold whole in memory. A file that can't be read or isn't one of those is BadSound. Needs Audio and Fs.read
audio.voice(volume: 1.0, pan: 0.0) -> Voice ! AudioErrorA voice the program fills with sound it makes, 48,000 frames a second: for synthesizers and sound made as a game runs. Needs Audio
voice.fill(state, next) -> SFor a voice from audio.voice: makes as many frames as it has room for, about 50 ms ahead, each by next: fn(S) -> (S, Float32, Float32) (the state after the frame, then its left and right), from state; gives the state after them. Needs Audio
voice.stop()Stops it at once. Needs Audio, as do the three below
voice.set_volume(volume), voice.set_pan(pan)Changes it while it plays
voice.is_playing() -> BoolWhether it still plays: false once it ended or was stopped
Function or methodGives
audio.listen() -> Recording ! AudioErrorStarts recording what the system's default microphone hears, at its own rate and with its first two channels, and gives at once. Needs Microphone
rec.take() -> SoundWhat it heard since the last take, or since it started, as a sound; an empty one when nothing new. Between takes it keeps up to 60 seconds; past that, what it hears is dropped until the next take. Needs Microphone, as stop does
rec.stop()Stops recording; a take after it gives what was left. Letting a Recording go stops it too
audio.record(seconds) -> Sound ! AudioErrorRecords for seconds (more than 0, at most 3,600), waiting meanwhile, and gives the sound. Needs Microphone
