A time of day the clocks skipped when daylight saving time began moves later by the gap, as RFC 5545 does; resolve gives both moments.14.4workszones-move-a-skipped-time-later
A Zone from time.zone(name) gives the date and time its clocks show at a moment, daylight saving time included.14.4workszones-show-local-time
A time of day the clocks showed twice when daylight saving time ended is its earlier moment, as RFC 5545 does; resolve gives both.14.4workszones-take-a-repeated-time-earlier
time.zone(name) fails with UnknownZone(name) for a name the IANA database doesn't have.14.4workszones-unknown-name-fails
